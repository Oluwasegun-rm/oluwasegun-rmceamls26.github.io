what_i_learned: |
  Today was dedicated to preparing for our final presentation at the Summer AI Research Institute Symposium. My team and I spent the day practicing our presentation, reviewing each section of our slides, and making revisions to improve the overall flow and clarity of our research story. We worked to ensure that our project's motivation, methodology, implementation, results, and conclusions were presented in a way that would be easy for the audience to follow.

  Throughout our practice sessions, we refined how we explained technical concepts such as our proposed Signal Gated Omic Fusion (SGOF) framework, the motivation behind addressing modality dominance, and the significance of our experimental findings. We also discussed how to communicate our work to an audience with varying levels of technical knowledge while still maintaining the accuracy of our research.

  As we practiced, we received feedback from our graduate mentor and teammates on both our slide content and presentation delivery. This helped us improve our transitions, strengthen our explanations, and become more confident in answering potential questions about the project. By the end of the day, I felt much more comfortable presenting our research and explaining the decisions we made throughout the implementation process.
